# Google Business Profile — Setup Plan

> **Date:** 2026-06-06
> **Goal:** Get Benjamin Tims / Ben Tims Media ranking in Google Maps + local Search
> for "real estate photographer Austin" (and nearby cities) and turn that into
> inquiries. Free. This is a **local-SEO foundation** — it pairs with Google Search
> Console and the on-site `LocalBusiness` structured data (NAP must match across all).

---

## 0. Decisions to lock first (consistency = ranking)

Your **NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) must be identical everywhere — site, GBP,
directories. Decide these before creating the profile:

- **Business name:** the public-facing name, used verbatim everywhere. (e.g. "Ben Tims
  Media" or "BTP Photography".) **Do not** stuff keywords like "Austin Real Estate
  Photographer" into the name — it violates Google guidelines and risks suspension.
- **Phone number:** a number you'll answer (Google Voice number is fine if you'd
  rather not use your personal cell publicly).
- **Service area, not address:** you travel to clients, so run it as a **service-area
  business** and **hide your home address**. List the cities you cover.
- **Primary category:** **Photographer** (closest official category). Add secondary
  categories like **Commercial photographer** if available.
- **Website:** `https://bentimsmedia.com`

Suggested service area (edit to reality): Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown,
Pflugerville, Leander, San Marcos, Kyle, Buda, Dripping Springs.

---

## 1. Create the profile

1. Use (or create) a dedicated **business Google account** — not a personal one you
   might lose access to. (e.g. a Gmail you control for the business.)
2. Go to **google.com/business → "Manage now."**
3. **Business name:** enter the exact public name from §0.
4. **Category:** choose **Photographer** as primary.
5. **Storefront?** Choose **No** → it becomes a **service-area business**.
6. **Service area:** add the cities from §0.
7. **Contact:** add your phone + `https://bentimsmedia.com`.
8. **Finish & verify** — pick the offered method (video verification is common for
   service-area businesses; phone/text or postcard may also appear). Verification can
   take a few minutes to a couple weeks depending on method.

---

## 2. Complete the profile fully (Google rewards completeness)

- **Description (≤750 chars):** natural, specific, mentions real estate + short-term
  rental photography + Greater Austin + walkthrough video. No keyword stuffing.
- **Services:** add each as its own service with a short description:
  - Real estate listing photography
  - Airbnb / short-term rental photography
  - Gimbal walkthrough video / social clips
- **Hours:** set realistic hours (or "open by appointment").
- **Photos:** add a **logo**, a strong **cover photo**, and 10–15 of your **best real
  listing shots** (interiors + exteriors). This is a photography business — photos are
  your conversion. Add your real work as you build the portfolio.
- **Attributes / messaging:** enable messaging if you'll respond; set any relevant
  attributes.

---

## 3. Reviews (the biggest ranking + trust lever)

- After each job, ask the **agent / homeowner / host** to leave a Google review — get
  your short review link from the dashboard and send it in your delivery email.
- **Respond to every review** (thank positive ones; address any negative calmly).
- Aim for a steady trickle, not a one-time burst.

---

## 4. Keep it active

- Post a **Google Update** occasionally (new listing shot, a tip, availability).
- Add fresh photos regularly.
- Keep hours/contact accurate.

---

## 5. Connect it back to the website (later code task)

- Link the site → GBP (e.g. a "Find us on Google" link / review button).
- Add **`LocalBusiness` structured data** on the site with the *same* NAP + service
  area (a separate to-do in the foundations list).
- Optionally embed a Google Map on the Contact page.

---

## 6. Measure

Use **GBP performance/insights**: profile views, searches you appear for, calls,
direction requests, and website clicks. Cross-reference with Google Search Console and
site analytics to see what's driving inquiries.

---

## Pitfalls to avoid

- ❌ Keyword-stuffing the business **name** (suspension risk).
- ❌ A fake/borrowed **address** — use service-area mode instead.
- ❌ Duplicate profiles — keep **one** profile only.
- ❌ Inconsistent NAP across site/GBP/directories.

> Deeper local-SEO / marketing research (citations, directories, review strategy) can
> come later — this plan is the proper foundational setup. NAP details chosen here
> should feed the on-site `LocalBusiness` schema task.
