## Future work (the real pricing project — do later)

1. **Austin, TX greater-area market research.** Benchmark each category against what
   local photographers actually charge (real estate, portrait, event, maternity) so
   the numbers make sense for the market. Greater Austin metro (incl. Round Rock,
   Cedar Park, Georgetown, San Marcos) — note that real-estate pricing often scales
   by **square footage / photo count / turnaround time**, which our tiers should
   reflect.
2. **How to calculate pricing (framework to apply):**
   - **Cost of Doing Business (CODB):** gear, software/CRM, insurance, travel,
     editing time, taxes, marketing — annualized.
   - **Target income + billable capacity:** desired salary ÷ realistic # of paid
     jobs/year = minimum revenue per job.
   - **Time per job:** shoot + travel + editing/culling + admin — price the *whole*
     job, not just the shoot hour (real estate editing is significant).
   - **Competitor benchmarking:** position vs. the Austin rates from step 1
     (budget / mid / premium).
   - **Value-based adjustments:** rush turnaround, twilight/drone, video add-ons,
     volume/recurring-agent discounts.
   - Sanity-check against PPA / industry benchmarks.
3. **Validate the real-estate tiers first** (that's what's live) — confirm photo
   counts, turnaround, and the +$150 video add-on are competitive in Austin.
