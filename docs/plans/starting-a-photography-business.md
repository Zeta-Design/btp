> creators). **Date:** 2026-06-06.
> **How to read this doc:** the **Next Steps** (top) and the **Core Plan** are *distilled from*

> any S-corp election with a **qualified CPA/attorney** (ideally one who specializes in photographers).

## Next steps (do these first, in order)

1. **Pick ONE niche / "money lane" and your ideal client, and write down your monthly number** (the
   revenue you need to live + run the business). One niche is easier to market, be known for, and
   scale; expand later. *(Kylee 3:02; Carty-LOW 1:18; Sarah 2:14.)*
2. **Open a separate business bank account and start tracking every dollar in and out** (a free
   spreadsheet is fine to start). **Set aside 25–30% of every payment for taxes** in a separate
   account you don't touch. *(Nadia 14:16; Heather 3:26; Luke-DTWM 2:37; Tiffany 22:43.)*
3. **Build a focused portfolio by doing 2–3 shoots FREE (not cheap) of the exact work you want to be
   hired for**, and collect a strong testimonial from each. Shooting *cheap* brands you as cheap;
   shooting *free for portfolio* protects your future price. *(Carty Top-3 4:35; Cameron 4:28–5:03;
   Tracy-Pricing 3:32.)*
4. **Set profitable package prices** based on *total* time + full costs (not just shooting hours),
   and **require a signed contract + a non-refundable deposit before every shoot.** *(Katja 0:59;
   Sarah 4:12; Tracy-Essentials 5:10.)*
5. **Stand up a simple site + a CRM** with a contact form, templated booking emails, and
   Stripe/Square (not personal Venmo). Don't over-invest in the website. *(Kylee 7:42; Cameron 5:31;
   Chris 6:57.)* → see the companion note **`docs/research/photography-crm-selection.md`**.
6. **Start one marketing engine you OWN:** blog every shoot with local/venue SEO keywords and collect
   emails; build referral relationships. **Skip paid ads for now.** *(Kylee 11:58; Thin Angle 4:29;
   Nathan 12:41; Chris 4:47.)*
7. **Book a one-time consult with a photographer-specialist CPA once income is consistent** — and
   *before* considering an S-corp, multi-state work, or big gear purchases. *(Nadia 13:32; Travis
   "When… Skip an Accountant"; Luke-DTWM 1:02.)*

## Core plan (phased)

### Phase 0 — Decide & frame (before spending money)
- **Choose one niche** by passion × skill × *local* market demand × competition, and check it's
  durable, not a fad. Genres have very different economics (commercial > weddings/portrait;
  real-estate = low barrier + steady volume). *(Carty-Start 5:02; Carty Top-3; Jerad 8:00.)*
- **Know your "nut"** (monthly $ needed) and work backwards: fewer higher-value clients beat volume
  (50 × \$2,000 = 500 × \$200, but a fraction of the work). *(Carty-LOW 1:18; Sarah 2:14; Katja 11:41.)*
- **Pick a delivery model up front**, especially for portrait: digital-files-commodity vs.
  in-person-sales of printed wall art/albums. *(Sarah "Portrait Pricing" 0:53–8:00.)*

### Phase 1 — Build the proof (skills + portfolio)
- **Get reps:** treat practice shoots like paid jobs; shoot friends/events to get comfortable with
  people. *(Cameron 4:28; Sergio-Gigs 4:16.)*
- **Curate a cohesive portfolio of the work you want to be hired for** — quality over quantity; don't
  show work you don't want to repeat. *(Carty-Start step 5; Tracy-Essentials 1:53; Kylee 2:25.)*
- **Land the first clients with free-for-portfolio (never cheap):** 2–3 free shoots, set the
  expectation it's for portfolio, get a testimonial, then ask "what would you have paid?" to anchor
  real prices. Real-estate variant: open-house sample photos / "Trojan-horse" free shoot for agents.
  *(Carty Top-3 4:35; Cameron 5:59; Eli-3things 3:51; Cineminn 4:20.)*

### Phase 2 — Set up the business (money & legal — Tier-1 CPA territory)
- **You're a sole proprietor the moment you take money** (even \$1); report it on Schedule C. No
  registration needed. *(Tiffany 13:12; Nadia 10:24.)*
- **An LLC does NOT lower your taxes** — it's legal/liability protection. Don't stack entities on a
  tiny business; complexity costs money and focus. *(Jasmine "Why an LLC Won't Reduce…" 0:55, 4:26;
  Nadia 46:04; Tiffany 15:25.)*
- **Separate bank account from day one**; keep business and personal money apart (also protects the
  LLC veil later). *(Nadia 14:16; Tiffany 16:08; Travis-Pay 0:49; Luke-DTWM 2:37.)*
- **Quarterly estimated taxes:** set aside ~25–30% (some say 30–45%) and pay quarterly to avoid the
  underpayment penalty. **Safe harbor** = pay 100% of last year's tax (110% if AGI > \$150k) in 4
  installments. *(Heather 3:26; Nadia 15:54; Jasmine "Save 50k" 7:09; Tiffany-Pay 3:06; Travis-Every-Tax.)*
- **Track income + ALL expenses** to lower taxable income: gear, **mileage (keep a LOG — no log = IRS
  disallows all car expenses; 2026 rate \$0.72/mi)**, **home office (exclusive + regular use → also
  converts commuting miles to business miles)**, software, education/courses, % of utilities/rent.
  Keep receipts **3–7 years**. *(Nadia 20:46 / 18:20 / 12:20; Tiffany 39:47; Katey 1:11; Heather 2:16.)*
- **Keep tooling simple:** a spreadsheet or QuickBooks is enough for a simple business — don't buy
  complex software you won't use. *(Luke-Taxes 6:51; Jasmine "LLC" 2:01; Rebecca 2:11.)*
- **Sales tax varies by state and you may have to collect/remit it; many states now tax digital
  products.** Multi-state/destination work multiplies this. **Confirm with a CPA.** *(Nadia 24:18;
  Tiffany 6:39 / 44:47; Travis-Every-Tax 4:13.)*
- **Get a CPA when** income is consistent / you go S-corp / multi-state / make big purchases / hit
  capacity. *(Travis "When… Skip an Accountant"; Nadia 13:32; Luke-DTWM 1:02.)*
- **S-corp is a LATER move, not day one:** only worth it when profit consistently far exceeds a
  reasonable salary (rule of thumb ~\$2,500–\$3,500/mo profit). It requires payroll (Gusto/ADP) and a
  1120S return; done wrong it *raises* tax. *(Tiffany 18:54; Jasmine "LLC vs S-Corp" 4:13 / "Save 50k"
  0:35; Travis-Every-Tax 1:45.)*
- **Contract + deposit before every shoot** (covers cancellation, payment, scope). *(Tracy-Essentials
  5:10; Carty-Start step 3.)*

### Phase 3 — Make booking & delivery easy (systems set up *before* the first client)
- **Simple website + clear contact form** (one-page Squarespace/Pixieset/Pixpa is fine — don't
  over-invest). *(Cameron 5:31; Tracy-Essentials 1:53; Chris 6:57.)*
- **A CRM** for packages, contracts, invoices, payments, and templated workflow emails — set up the
  whole client journey before booking anyone, so every client gets the same experience. Real-estate
  has its own stack (SPIQ/Spiyo, CubiCasa). *(Kylee 7:42 / 9:50; Eli-What 15:14; Luke-DTWM 1:26.)*
  → tool choice in **`docs/research/photography-crm-selection.md`**.
- **Payments via Stripe/Square/PayPal**, not personal Venmo. *(Kylee 11:16.)*
- **A welcome/pricing pack with boundaries** (business hours, what's included vs. extra) to filter
  clients and stop scope creep; **back up files in two places.** *(Tracy-Essentials 7:46 / 10:54;
  Kylee 5:30.)*

### Phase 4 — Price for profit
- **Price for TOTAL time + full Cost of Doing Business**, not shooting hours (a "3-hour shoot" is
  15–19 hours of real work). *(Katja 0:59 / 3:49; Tracy-Pricing 3:32; Sarah "Portrait" 0:53.)*
- **Be profitable from day one** — don't "wait until you're good enough to charge more." Sarah's
  formula: cost-of-sales (incl. your time) ≤ 20%, overhead 30–40%, net profit 40–50%. *(Sarah
  "If I Started" 4:12; Tiffany; Carty-Start 9:53.)*
- **Use packages with an anchor price and upsell at booking** to raise average order value;
  real-estate especially: bundle video/drone/floor plans/twilight to escape the ~\$300 commodity
  ceiling. *(Eli-3things 5:04 / 6:24; SnapHome-Start 10:36; Sarah two-album 11:09.)*
- **Portrait: sell printed wall art via in-person sales, not digital files.** *(Sarah "Portrait" 3:31.)*
- **Sell the transformation and the story; anchor price to what they already spend.** *(Sarah
  "Portrait" 13:23 / 15:38; Carty-LOW 2:08; Katja 12:53 test prices.)*

### Phase 5 — Market & own your audience (owned assets beat rented reach)
- **Blog every shoot with local + venue SEO keywords** — "searchable is profitable," and posts
  compound for years; venue/long-tail posts are the #1 forum-recommended SEO play. Fix H1/title
  tags/category pages/schema. *(Kylee 11:58; Joy 2:26; Thin Angle 4:29 / full SEO list; Nathan 12:41
  MLS visibility.)*
- **Build an email list early** and email weekly/biweekly — it's an asset; social is a "hamster
  wheel." *(Kylee 15:14; Carty-Start 24:39.)*
- **Relationships & referrals > followers:** networking (BNI/Chamber), partner with businesses that
  serve your client, target the *right* clients (e.g. top ~10–20% of realtors). *(Sarah "If I Started"
  11:07; Carty-Start 26:01; Eli-3things 1:46; Cineminn 5:34.)*
- **Word-of-mouth is the cheapest growth → deliver an exceptional client experience and go the extra
  mile** (respond fast, small bonuses). *(Carty-Start 29:28; Nathan 12:41 ad budget → \$0; Carty-Secret
  6:19.)*
- **Don't cargo-cult or over-spend on ads/tools you don't need.** *(Chris 4:47: \$650/mo agency ads →
  0 bookings, all bookings came organically; Nathan 12:41; Luke-DTWM 9:15 debit-card discipline.)*
- **Daily revenue-generating activity:** every day, do one thing that brings a potential client value.
  *(Carty-LOW 3:42.)*
- **Define brand values and qualify for FIT; say no to mismatches** (do a consult call). A "bad client"
  is usually a bad *fit*. *(Luke "BAD Clients" throughout; Sarah assumption-of-sale 9:02.)*

### Phase 6 — Grow & protect
- **Diversify / niche-stack once established** (Jerad: AI takes commodity work; authentic human work is
  growing). *(Jerad 8:33; Carty Top-3 9:38; Nathan 15:16 architectural + listing.)*
- **Outsource the bottleneck (editing) — labor arbitrage**; AI tools cut post time hugely. *(Cineminn
  2:43 ~\$0.40/photo; Sergio "Capture One"; Grace/CNBC contractors.)*
- **Hire help / a second shooter at capacity.** *(SnapHome-Start; CNBC-Grace 6:51; Lin & Jirsa team.)*
- **Protect your life from the start** — set work hours/boundaries, avoid burnout (volume kills margin
  and joy). *(Kylee 17:58; CNBC-Grace 6:51 scaled 46 → 25 weddings; Sarah margin > volume.)*
- **Persist — year one is often a loss; don't quit or take shortcuts.** *(Nathan 9:51 / 22:02; Cameron
  7:39; Jerad.)*

## How this was weighed — the evidence framework

Not all of these 45 sources deserve equal trust. Weight each claim by:

> **weight ≈ (credential in the *relevant* domain) × (track record + specificity of real numbers) ÷ (conflict of interest)** — and only treat something as a *rule* when **≥2 independent sources agree.**

## Research findings (the source of truth — by theme, with citations)

> Citations are `(Speaker — video, timestamp)`. Tiers are per the framework above.

### A. Money, taxes & structure — **Tier 1 consensus**
- **Sole-proprietor automatically on first dollar; Schedule C** (Tiffany 13:12; Nadia 10:24).
- **Self-employment tax = 15.3%**; W-2 withholding doesn't cover side income (Heather 1:31; Travis-Every-Tax 1:03).
- **Quarterly estimates + safe harbor (100%/110% prior-year)**; underpayment penalty is non-deductible (Jasmine "Save 50k" 7:09; Nadia 15:54; Travis-Every-Tax 2:52).
- **LLC = liability protection, not a tax cut**; don't over-structure early (Jasmine "Why an LLC…" 0:55/4:26; Nadia 46:04; Tiffany 15:25).
- **S-corp later, only when profit ≫ reasonable salary** (~\$2.5–3.5k/mo); needs payroll + 1120S; can backfire with financed assets/third-party debt (Jasmine "LLC vs S-Corp" 4:13 + "Save 50k" 0:35; Tiffany 18:54; Travis-Pay 0:57).
- **Deductions:** gear, **mileage w/ log** (\$0.72/mi 2026), **home office (exclusive use → business miles)**, software, education, % utilities; **no** deduction for donations or everyday clothing; meals only 50% and only with a business contact (Nadia 18:20/20:46/46:54; Tiffany 39:47).
- **Records:** separate bank account; keep receipts 3–7 yrs; scan/organize (Nadia 12:20/14:16; Tiffany 24:39; Luke-DTWM 10:21).
- **Sales/use tax varies by state; digital increasingly taxed; you become a collector**; back-file to LLC start date if behind (VDA) (Nadia 24:18; Tiffany 6:39/11:51/44:47; Travis-Every-Tax 4:13).
- **Pay yourself:** owner-draw (sole prop/LLC, not a deduction) vs. payroll (S-corp); pay consistently to know cash needs → price accordingly (Travis-Pay; Tiffany-Pay 0:37).
- **A CRM is not accounting** and **don't over-tool** a simple business — spreadsheet/QuickBooks is enough early (Luke-Taxes 6:51; Rebecca 2:11). *(Aligns with the CRM note's CRM-+-QuickBooks finding.)*
- **Conflicted but substantive:** Evan (outsource bookkeeping — Core Group ad), Heather/Katey (tracking — sell spreadsheets).

### B. Niche & positioning
- **Specialize / pick one "money lane"** — easier to market, command price, be searchable; expand later (Kylee 3:02; Cameron 3:27; Carty-Secret 2:13 generalist-vs-specialist 10× gap).
- **Choose by passion × skill × local demand × competition × durability** (Carty-Start 5:02).
- **Avoid over-saturated local lanes; fill a gap** (Sergio-Gigs 5:21; SnapHome-Start 2:20).
- **Diversify once established** so one market shock doesn't reset you (Jerad 8:33).

### C. Pricing
- **Price for total time + full CODB, not shooting hours** (Katja 0:59/3:49; Tracy-Pricing 3:32).
- **Margin > volume; profitable from day one** (Sarah "Portrait" 0:53 + "If I Started" 4:12; Carty-Start 9:53).
- **Free-for-portfolio, never cheap** — cheap brands you cheap (Carty Top-3 4:35; Tracy-Pricing 0:00 the \$50 story).
- **Packages + anchor price + upsell at booking → higher AOV** (Eli-3things 5:04; SnapHome-Start 10:36; Sarah two-album 11:09).
- **Charge for usage/licensing on commercial/B2B work** (Katja 6:07).
- **Sell value/story; anchor to existing spend; test prices** (Sarah "Portrait" 13:23/15:38; Katja 12:53).

### D. Portfolio & first clients
- **Reps + cohesive, niche-specific portfolio of work you want to repeat** (Cameron 4:28/5:03; Carty-Start step 5; Tracy-Essentials 1:53).
- **Outreach without hard-selling; offer free portfolio sessions; nail the work** (Cameron 5:59).
- **Shadow seasoned pros; build vendor/planner relationships** (Carty Top-3 5:43).

### E. Systems (booking / CRM / contracts)
- **Make booking easy: contact form → CRM → templated workflow set up before the first client** (Kylee 7:42/9:50).
- **Contracts + deposits non-negotiable; welcome pack prevents scope creep; back up files 2×** (Tracy-Essentials 5:10/7:46/10:54).
- **CRMs named:** HoneyBook (Kylee), Studio Ninja (Luke), Dubsado/17hats/Pixieset (Kylee), Lemley (Chris), Spiyo/SPIQ + CubiCasa for real estate (Eli-What 15:14). *(Cross-ref the CRM-selection note.)*

### F. Marketing & owning your audience
- **Blog + local/venue SEO compounds for years; "searchable is profitable"** (Kylee 11:58; Joy 2:26; Thin Angle: H1 = "[city] wedding photographer", separate category pages, keyword-first title tags, venue blogs, schema).
- **Email list early; weekly/biweekly** (Kylee 15:14).
- **Relationships/referrals/networking > followers; target the right clients** (Sarah "If I Started" 11:07; Eli-3things 1:46 top realtors; Carty-Start 26:01).
- **Word-of-mouth + exceptional service; respond within the hour** (Carty-Start 29:28; Nathan 12:41 → \$0 ad budget).
- **Don't waste money cargo-culting ads/tools** (Chris 4:47 \$650/mo → 0 weddings; all organic).
- **Magnetic messaging / daily client-getting activity** (Carty-LOW 2:08/3:42; Joy copy).

### G. Client experience & fit
- **Define brand values; qualify for fit; say no; consult call** — a bad client is a bad fit (Luke "BAD Clients" throughout).
- **Assumption of sale: explain price/process up front** (Sarah "If I Started" 9:02; Sarah "Portrait" 3:31).

### H. Gear & editing (don't over-invest)
- **Skill > gear; buy what makes/saves money; buy used; quality is the differentiator** (Nathan 19:26 shoots a "consumer" Z5; SnapHome-Start 6:08 "iPhone could do it"; Carty-Start step 4).
- **Outsource editing (labor arbitrage); AI tools slash post time** (Cineminn 2:43; Sergio "Capture One").

### I. Niche deep-dive — real estate (low barrier, steady, scalable)
- **Steady work, six figures from ~2 shoots/day if priced right** (Nathan 17:56; Jerad 2:41 growing market).
- **Cheap used kit (~\$700): body + wide lens + sturdy tripod; add drone/360/video later** (Cineminn 0:50; Eli-What 1:13).
- **Build portfolio via agent open-house / free shoots; "Trojan-horse"** (Cineminn 4:20; SnapHome-Start 9:07).
- **Sell speed/reliability; bundle services (photo/drone/floor-plan/twilight/video/3D) to beat the \$300 ceiling; anchor package** (Cineminn 5:34; Eli-3things 5:04; SnapHome-Start 10:36).
- **Target the serious ~10–20% of realtors with consistent listings** (Eli-3things 1:46; Nathan 15:00).
- **MLS visibility → referrals → near-zero ad spend** (Nathan 12:41).
- **Tools:** CubiCasa (phone floor plans), Spiyo/SPIQ (ordering/scheduling/delivery), Zillow/Matterport 3D (Eli-What 6:08/15:14). *(Eli's entity-tax aside is Tier-3 — defer to Tier 1 in §A.)*

### J. Market reality & case study
- **Industry is growing, not dying; AI takes only commodity work** (Jerad 0:55: global \$55.6B→\$81.8B; weddings/real-estate/product/food growing; 7:26 AI can't capture reality). Median photographer income ~\$33k (75% part-time); wedding \$40–100k+, commercial \$50–120k+ (Jerad 7:52).
- **Case study — Grace Torres (CNBC), 23, Lakeland FL:** \$177k/yr; weddings start \$4k, avg \$4.5–5.5k; grew \$0→\$500→\$1.5k→\$7k→full-time; invested early in **contracts + a CPA**; scaled 46 → 25 weddings to avoid burnout; bookings now from **referrals**, not social. (CNBC 4:02/6:51.)

## Definition of done (for the new photographer)
- Niche + ideal client + monthly number written down.
- Separate business bank account open; income/expenses tracked; 25–30% set aside for taxes; quarterly estimates scheduled.
- Portfolio of the target work built (free-for-portfolio, with testimonials).
- Profitable package pricing set; contract + deposit required before shoots.
- Website + CRM + payment + templated workflow live (see CRM note).
- One owned marketing engine running (SEO blog + email list + referral relationships); no paid ads yet.
- CPA consult booked once income is consistent; S-corp deferred until the profit threshold is met.
